    Tips for scanning

    Lay the tiles in one straight row with small gaps between them.

    Fill the frame with the tiles and keep the phone parallel to them.

    Use good, even light. Turn on the torch if the room is dim.

    Tap Hold to freeze a reading, and tap the tiles to fix a letter.

    At the end of a game, switch to Count tiles and scan each player’s tiles a few rows at a time. Add each batch to the tally, then save it as that player’s score.
