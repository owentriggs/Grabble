    Tips for scanning

    Lay the tiles in one straight row with small gaps between them.

    Fill the frame with the tiles and keep the phone parallel to them.

    Use good, even light. Turn on the torch if the room is dim.

    If a letter comes out wrong, tap the tiles to fix it.

    Turn on Live to read the tiles continuously, without pressing the shutter.

    What the scanner saw
